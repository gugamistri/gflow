How it works

From screenshot to link in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Capture with the extension

    Install the Chrome extension and click through your product. Every click becomes a demo screen, with the clicked spot marked.

  2. 2

    Edit with AI and narration

    Adjust highlights and copy in the editor. AI generates titles and descriptions for each step, and creates matching voice narration.

  3. 3

    Deliver and track

    Send the link, embed it on the client's site and see who opened it, who reached the end and where people stop.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does my data live?

On the Free plan, projects are stored locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers unless you publish a temporary link. Published tours remain active for 7 days via temporary links, then are deleted. On Pro, projects, permanent links and leads live on the GuiaFlow server, tied to your account.

Does GuiaFlow comply with the LGPD (Brazil's data protection law)?

We follow the LGPD: we keep the minimum needed for the account and links, use no advertising trackers and never sell data. Lead capture asks the visitor for explicit consent. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is GuiaFlow open source?

Yes. The editor and the player are MIT-licensed and the code is at github.com/gugamistri/gflow.

Do I need a credit card?

Not for Free: no account and no card, just open the editor. GuiaFlow Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription billed through Stripe, and you can cancel anytime.

Does the person receiving the demo need to install anything?

No. The demo opens in the browser, on mobile or desktop, from the link or from your site. Only the person creating the demo needs the Chrome extension.

Is GuiaFlow GDPR-compliant?

GuiaFlow is built to be privacy-friendly, but we don't claim any GDPR certification (there isn't an official one). On the Free plan, projects stay in your browser and aren't sent to us unless you publish a temporary link, which is deleted after 7 days. Tour analytics use no cookies and don't store IP addresses, and they respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Lead capture records the visitor's explicit consent. We're based in Brazil and operate under Brazil's LGPD. Some providers are in the United States (see our Privacy Policy for the full list). If you collect leads from people in the EU, you are the controller of that data; contact contato@guiaflow.pro with questions about using GuiaFlow under the GDPR.

Can I deliver the demo under my client's brand?

On Pro, the player runs without the GuiaFlow badge. It is not white-label: the domain stays guiaflow.pro and there is no custom logo.

Does the lead go to my client's CRM?

Yes, on Pro: LeadPronto (OAuth), RD Station Marketing, HubSpot or a signed webhook to Make, n8n, Zapier, Pipefy, Pipedrive and Google Sheets. The lead carries name, email, phone, consent, UTMs, source and which form it came from (start or end of the tour).

Do I need access to the client's product?

You need the screens. Capture with the extension using the account the client enables, or upload the screenshots they send. GuiaFlow does not access the product on its own.
