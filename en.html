How it works

From screens and photos to a link in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Capture or upload

    Use the Chrome extension to capture a product's screens, or upload photos and screenshots. Each image becomes a guide step or a gallery photo.

  2. 2

    Choose gallery or guide

    Organize into sections and adjust the text. In a guide, AI can write the steps and the narration comes from the same text.

  3. 3

    Share and follow up

    Send the link or embed it on your site. On Pro, see who opened it, who reached the end and where people stop.

Features

Features to show and follow up

Photo gallery

Full-screen photos with zoom, sections and thumbnails. Viewers browse freely, with no balloons or narration.

Step-by-step guide

Balloons, highlights and a simulated click over your screenshots. Viewers move at their own pace or on autoplay.

Contact capture Pro 👑

If you want, ask for a name, email or phone number at the first step, with viewer consent. Contacts stay in the editor and export to CSV.

Embed on your site Pro 👑

One snippet for HTML, WordPress or Webflow. The presentation adapts to the page width.

WhatsApp and contact at the end

The last step takes viewers to your WhatsApp, a custom link, or a contact form (Pro 👑) that lands in your inbox.

AI copy and narration

Generate each step's copy and, in guides, turn it into narration. With Pro 👑 the AI is included; on Free, use your own key.

Permanent links Pro 👑

An address that never expires, with a cover image in WhatsApp and LinkedIn previews. Update the presentation and the link stays the same.

Visit analytics Pro 👑

See how many people opened it, how many reached the end, who clicked the final button and at which step people stop.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

Not to use the free editor. Pro features use an account.

Are a gallery and a guide the same thing?

They are two modes of the same editor: the gallery highlights photos and details; the guide organizes an explanation into steps.

Do I have to collect contacts?

No. You decide whether to include capture and what the viewer's next step will be.

Is the “Create with help” assistant paid?

No. The assistant is free and works without an account. AI suggestions use your own AI key (no cost on GuiaFlow) or GuiaFlow's hosted AI, included in Pro. Without AI, you build everything by hand and review before publishing.

Where does my data live?

On the Free plan, projects are stored locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers unless you publish a temporary link. Published tours remain active for 7 days via temporary links, then are deleted. On Pro, projects, permanent links and leads live on the GuiaFlow server, tied to your account.

Does GuiaFlow comply with the LGPD (Brazil's data protection law)?

We follow the LGPD: we keep the minimum needed for the account and links, use no advertising trackers and never sell data. Lead capture asks the visitor for explicit consent. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is GuiaFlow open source?

Yes. The editor and the player are MIT-licensed and the code is at github.com/gugamistri/gflow.

Do I need a credit card?

Not for Free: no account and no card, just open the editor. GuiaFlow Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription billed through Stripe, and you can cancel anytime.

Does the person receiving the presentation need to install anything?

No. The presentation opens in the browser, on mobile or desktop, from the link or from your site. Only people capturing screens need the Chrome extension.

Is GuiaFlow GDPR-compliant?

GuiaFlow is built to be privacy-friendly, but we don't claim any GDPR certification (there isn't an official one). On the Free plan, projects stay in your browser and aren't sent to us unless you publish a temporary link, which is deleted after 7 days. Tour analytics use no cookies and don't store IP addresses, and they respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Lead capture records the visitor's explicit consent. We're based in Brazil and operate under Brazil's LGPD. Some providers are in the United States (see our Privacy Policy for the full list). If you collect leads from people in the EU, you are the controller of that data; contact contato@guiaflow.pro with questions about using GuiaFlow under the GDPR.

Can I deliver it with my client's brand?

On Pro, the player runs without the GuiaFlow badge. It is not white-label: the domain stays guiaflow.pro and there is no custom logo.

Can contacts go to a CRM?

If you turn on capture, Pro lets you connect LeadPronto (OAuth), RD Station Marketing, HubSpot or a signed webhook to Make, n8n, Zapier, Pipefy, Pipedrive and Google Sheets. The contact carries name, email, phone, consent, UTMs, source and which form it came from (start or end of the tour).

Do I need access to the client's product?

You need the screens. Capture with the extension using the account the client enables, or upload the screenshots they send. GuiaFlow does not access the product on its own.
